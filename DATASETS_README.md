# FarmConnect Datasets and Sources

This document provides links and descriptions for the primary datasets and information sources used in the FarmConnect application.

## 1. Government Schemes & Loans (Kisan Suvidha)
*   **Source:** [Kisan Suvidha Portal](https://kisansuvidha.gov.in/)
*   **Description:** Official data for Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN), Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), and other central/state agricultural schemes.

## 2. Market Prices & Trends (Agmarknet)
*   **Source:** [Agmarknet - Agricultural Marketing Information Network](https://agmarknet.gov.in/)
*   **Description:** Real-time data on Mandi prices, commodity arrivals, and market trends across India.

## 3. Crop Scientific Data (ICAR)
*   **Source:** [Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)](https://icar.org.in/)
*   **Description:** Technical specifications for Indian crops, including soil suitability (Alluvial, Black, Red, etc.), duration, and nutrient requirements.

## 4. Weather & Climate Patterns (IMD)
*   **Source:** [India Meteorological Department (IMD)](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/)
*   **Description:** Historical and real-time data on regional rainfall, temperature ranges, and seasonal climate patterns across Indian states.

## 5. Expert Advisory & Support (mKisan)
*   **Source:** [mKisan Portal](https://mkisan.gov.in/)
*   **Description:** Contact information for Kisan Call Centres (KCC) and patterns for agricultural advisory services.

## 6. National Agriculture Statistics
*   **Source:** [Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare](https://agricoop.nic.in/)
*   **Description:** State-wise statistics on dominant crops, production yields, and agricultural land-use patterns.

## 7. Soil Classification Data
*   **Source:** [National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning (NBSS&LUP)](https://www.nbsslup.in/)
*   **Description:** Data on soil types and their distribution across the Indian subcontinent.

---
*Note: These datasets are used to power the AI Crop Advisor, Market Insights, and Regional Exploration tools within FarmConnect.*
