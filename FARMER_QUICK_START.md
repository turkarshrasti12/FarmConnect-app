# FarmConnect AI Features - Quick Start Guide

## 🐛 PEST DETECTION - Step by Step

### What Problems Does It Solve?
❌ "I see holes in my leaves but don't know what pest is causing it"
❌ "Is this a fungal disease or insect damage?"
❌ "I need proper treatment options, not guesses"

### ✅ How to Use Pest Detection

1. **Take a Clear Photo**
   - Use natural sunlight (avoid shadows)
   - Get close-up to see details
   - Capture the affected leaf/plant part
   - Include the damage and any visible insects

2. **Upload to FarmConnect**
   - Navigate to "Tools" or "Pest Advisor"
   - Click "Upload Image"
   - Select your photo

3. **Get Results**
   - AI analyzes visual characteristics
   - Shows pest/disease name with **confidence percentage**
   - Displays severity level

4. **Follow Treatment Plan**
   - Prevention measures (do first)
   - Organic treatment options
   - Chemical treatments (if severe)
   - Exact application timing

### 📋 Example: Armyworm Detection
```
IMAGE: Maize leaves with holes, caterpillar visible, brown droppings

RESULT:
✓ Pest: ARMYWORM
✓ Confidence: 95%
✓ Severity: High
✓ Action: Spray immediately

RECOMMENDED TREATMENT:
Organic: Bt spray @ 1-2 mL/L
Chemical: Flubendiamide @ 0.5 mL/L
Timing: Early morning (6-8 AM)
```

## 🌾 CROP ADVISOR - Step by Step

### What Problems Does It Solve?
❌ "What should I grow based on my land conditions?"
❌ "Will this crop work in my area?"
❌ "Which crop gives best profit in my region?"

### ✅ How to Use Crop Advisor

1. **Enter Your Farm Details**
   - **Location**: Select your state/district
   - **Season**: Kharif (Jun-Oct) or Rabi (Oct-Mar)
   - **Temperature**: Average in °C
   - **Rainfall**: Annual in mm
   - **Soil Type**: Loam, Clay, Sandy etc.
   - **Soil pH**: Between 5.5-8.5

2. **System Analyzes**
   - Checks climate compatibility
   - Assesses soil suitability
   - Considers water availability
   - Reviews market demand
   - Calculates risk factors

3. **Get Recommendations**
   - Top crops ranked by **accuracy** (90%+)
   - Expected yield per hectare
   - Water requirement
   - Profitability level
   - Specific management tips

4. **Compare Options**
   - Read why each crop is recommended
   - Check water/irrigation needs
   - Review risk factors and diseases
   - Compare profitability

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

### Pest Detection Tips:
1. ✓ Upload in **good lighting** (avoid indoor lights)
2. ✓ Get **close-up** of the affected area
3. ✓ Show **visible pests** if possible
4. ✓ Capture **brown droppings** or fungal spots
5. ✓ Include **healthy vs diseased** comparison if possible

2. ✓ **Soil pH** matters - get it tested if possible

## ❌ Common Issues & Solutions

### Pest Not Detected?
- **Issue**: Getting "Unknown" pest
- **Fix**: Upload clearer image with better lighting
- **Try**: Get closer to leaf, show pest if visible

### Crop Recommendation Seems Wrong?
- **Issue**: Crop not suitable for region
- **Fix**: Verify temperature, rainfall, soil data
- **Check**: Is soil pH in right range?

### Accuracy Too Low?
- **Issue**: Confidence <40%
- **Fix**: Upload multiple angles
- **Note**: System will suggest more info needed
